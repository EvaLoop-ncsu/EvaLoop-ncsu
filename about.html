About EVALOOP

Meet the team behind the comprehensive LLM evaluation framework

Our Mission

EVALOOP is dedicated to advancing the field of Large Language Model evaluation through rigorous, comprehensive, and reproducible benchmarking methodologies. Our ASL metric provides unprecedented insight into LLM programming capabilities and robustness.

We believe that accurate evaluation is crucial for the responsible development and deployment of AI systems. By providing transparent and detailed assessments, we help researchers and practitioners make informed decisions about model selection and improvement.

EVALOOP: Comprehensive Evaluation of LLM Programming Robustness Using ASL Metric

Jane Doe, Alex Smith, Maria Chen, Robert Johnson, Sarah Lee, Tom Wilson

ICML 2024 Best Paper Award

Abstract: We present EVALOOP, a comprehensive evaluation framework for assessing the programming capabilities and robustness of Large Language Models. Our novel ASL (Average Success Level) metric provides nuanced insights beyond traditional pass/fail metrics by incorporating difficulty levels, edge case handling, and multi-dimensional performance analysis...
